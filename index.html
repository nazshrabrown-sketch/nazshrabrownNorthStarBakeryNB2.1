Welcome to North Star Bakery

Step inside and breathe in the smell of fresh bread. We are a neighborhood bakery where every loaf, pastry, and cake is shaped by hand and shared with the community that makes us who we are. Whether you are grabbing breakfast on your commute, picking up dinner rolls for the family, or planning a big celebration, there is always a seat and a warm welcome for you.

What We’re Known For

  • Crusty artisan breads made with slow-fermented dough.
  • Buttery morning pastries that sell out before lunch.
  • Custom celebration cakes for birthdays, weddings, and events.
