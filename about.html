About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small home oven and a big wish: to give our neighbors a place that smells like comfort. Over the years it grew into a corner shop where regulars are greeted by name. We named it for the North Star because it helps people find their way home, and we hope our bakery does the same.

See How We Bake

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients. We keep our sourcing simple and local.

  • Flour from regional mills that support area farmers.
  • Eggs, butter, and honey from nearby family producers.
  • Seasonal fruit from neighborhood markets.
  • No artificial preservatives, ever.
